# Code of Conduct

PyIAMKit contributors are expected to collaborate professionally and respectfully.

We welcome technical disagreement, critical review and competing design proposals when they are expressed constructively and focus on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption and disclosure of private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may moderate or remove contributions that violate these expectations in order to keep the project safe and productive.
